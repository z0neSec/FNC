Vasiliy Ivanov

Builder. Soldier. Systems architect. Founder of Five Nines Consulting.

Background

Vasiliy spent years in the Airborne and Special Operations community before transitioning into technical infrastructure. That transition wasn't a pivot — it was a continuation of the same discipline applied to a different domain.

The skills that mattered in operations — clarity under pressure, planning for failure, executing without shortcuts — are the same skills that build resilient engineering systems. Vasiliy founded Five Nines Consulting in 2024 to bring that operational discipline to building technology products.

What He's Building

From operations to engineering.

Two parallel efforts define V's day-to-day work at FNC today.

FNC's Flagship Product

PromptSentry

V leads engineering on PromptSentry, FNC's AI security guardrail. The product protects AI applications from prompt injection, jailbreaks, PII leaks, and explicit content — used by educational platforms protecting kids and enterprises protecting their workforce.

In Development

Veteran Training Framework

V is designing the framework for a training program that will, over time, bring more veterans into engineering careers. The program is in active development — focused on hands-on technical mentorship, not credentialing or job placement.
