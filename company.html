Our Mission
& Story

FNC was founded by Vasiliy Ivanov on the idea that the discipline of military service belongs in the engineering room.

Today FNC is a small, focused team building technology products, with a long-term commitment to bringing more disciplined operators into engineering as the company grows.

The Tech Side

Our first product, PromptSentry, is an AI security guardrail used by platforms protecting kids and enterprises protecting their workforce. It exists because AI adoption is moving faster than the guardrails meant to protect the people using it.

The Human Side

Alongside our product work, FNC is building the framework for a veteran training program. Designed to bring veterans into high-impact technical roles through mentorship and learn-by-doing, we expect a meaningful share of our future engineering to come from veterans we've trained.
